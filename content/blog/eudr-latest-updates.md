Is the EUDR going to be delayed again? We found no proposal to change the dates as of 13 November 2026. The Commission's own EUDR page still lists 30 December 2026 as the start for large and medium operators.

The dates did not move this year. Almost everything around them did: the product list, the IT system, the guidance. This page covers what changed in 2026 and what it means for you. For the full history of the two earlier delays, see the [EUDR timeline](/blog/eudr-timeline).

**At a glance:**

- **30 December 2026**: the EUDR applies to large and medium operators and traders.
- **30 June 2027**: it applies to the remaining micro and small enterprises.
- **30 December 2027**: newly added products come into scope.
- The Commission's 4 May 2026 review left the application dates unchanged.
- Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/2102 rewrote the product list in Annex I. It entered into force on 18 September 2026.

## What changed in 2026

- **4 May 2026:** the Commission publishes its review report, [COM(2026) 191](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/document/download/a3c5c3a0-232e-43c4-b0b8-1eecb1df45c7_en?filename=Report+from+the+Commission+to+the+Council+and+Parliament+on+the+EUDR.pdf). It comes with updated guidance and FAQs and a draft delegated act on product scope. No new dates.
- **End of June 2026:** the EUDR Information System reopens after technical updates.
- **13 July 2026:** the Commission adopts the delegated act on product scope and an implementing act on the Information System. The second adds simplified declarations for micro and small primary operators and new specifications for the automated interfaces (APIs).
- **21 August 2026:** the Commission's FAQ on implementation carries this date. Check it before you settle a doubtful reading.
- **17 September 2026:** [Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/2102](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2026/2102/oj/eng) is published in the Official Journal. It enters into force the next day.

## The current dates

| Who | From |
|---|---|
| Large and medium operators and traders | 30 December 2026 |
| Micro and small enterprises already covered by the EU Timber Regulation | 30 December 2026 |
| Other micro and small enterprises | 30 June 2027 |
| Products newly added to Annex I | 30 December 2027 |

These come from the Commission's [EUDR page](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/topics/forests/deforestation/eu-deforestation-regulation-eudr_en) and its [13 July 2026 announcement](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/news/commission-updates-product-scope-and-tools-support-eudr-2026-07-13_en). The later June 2027 date has conditions. It covers operators only, not traders, and only businesses that already counted as micro or small on 31 December 2024. If you are unsure, plan for 30 December 2026.

## Will the EUDR be delayed again?

Not on current law. The 4 May 2026 report brought a simplification package, not a new date. On 13 July the Commission wrote that the regulation will begin to apply at the end of December.

In January 2026, Commissioner Roswall said the Commission did not favour reopening the regulation again, [according to Mayer Brown](https://www.mayerbrown.com/en/insights/publications/2026/02/eu-regulation-on-deforestation-free-products-eudr-what-lies-ahead-in-2026).

That is a signal, not a guarantee. Parliament and Council moved the dates twice in two years. Plan for the date and look again before the year ends.

## What the product list changes

The seven commodities stay the same. The list of derived products in Annex I does not.

| Removed | Added (from 30 December 2027) |
|---|---|
| Cattle hides, skins and leather | Soluble coffee |
| Re-treaded tyres | Certain palm oil derivatives |
| Soybeans for sowing | Frozen cattle tongues |
| Articles of vulcanised rubber | |
| Conveyor and transmission belts | |
| Aircraft and motor vehicle seats | |

The act also says that samples and products used for analysis or testing are out of scope. It adds targeted exemptions for waste, used and second-hand products, packing material, and products used to make medicines.

If you import soluble coffee or palm oil derivatives, you get a year more. If you import leather or rubber goods, check your CN codes against the new Annex I. [How to find a CN code](/blog/what-is-an-hs-code) explains the lookup.

## What has not changed

The duties are the same. Anyone who places a covered product on the EU market or exports it must show that it is deforestation-free and does not come from land cleared after 31 December 2020. It must also be legal under the law of the country of production. That evidence, including the geolocation of the plot, goes into a due-diligence statement in the EU Information System.

## What to do now

1. **Check your scope.** Compare your CN codes with Annex I as amended.
2. **Confirm your role.** Operator, trader or downstream operator? Your duties and your date depend on it. [Who is a downstream operator](/blog/eudr-downstream-operator) lays out the difference.
3. **Collect plot coordinates.** Tie each shipment to the land it came from.
4. **Test the Information System.** It has been open again since the end of June 2026.
5. **Ask suppliers early.** The data sits with them, and answers take time.

## FAQ

**What are the latest EUDR updates?**
The application dates are unchanged. In 2026 the Commission published its review report on 4 May, reopened the Information System in late June, and adopted a delegated act on product scope, in force since 18 September 2026.

Source: [European Commission: EUDR](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/topics/forests/deforestation/eu-deforestation-regulation-eudr_en)

**When does the EUDR apply now?**
From 30 December 2026 for large and medium operators and traders, and from 30 June 2027 for the remaining micro and small enterprises.

Source: [European Commission: 13 July 2026 announcement](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/news/commission-updates-product-scope-and-tools-support-eudr-2026-07-13_en)

**Will the EUDR be delayed again?**
We found no proposal to delay it as of 13 November 2026. The 4 May 2026 review left the dates alone. The legislators could still change them, as they did twice before.

Source: [European Commission: Report COM(2026) 191](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/document/download/a3c5c3a0-232e-43c4-b0b8-1eecb1df45c7_en?filename=Report+from+the+Commission+to+the+Council+and+Parliament+on+the+EUDR.pdf)

**Which products were added to or removed from the EUDR?**
Soluble coffee, certain palm oil derivatives and frozen cattle tongues were added. Cattle leather, re-treaded tyres, soybeans for sowing, vulcanised rubber articles, conveyor belts and vehicle seats were removed.

Source: [EUR-Lex: Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/2102](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2026/2102/oj/eng)

**When do the newly added products come into scope?**
From 30 December 2027, to give businesses time to prepare.

Source: [European Commission: 13 July 2026 announcement](https://environment.ec.europa.eu/news/commission-updates-product-scope-and-tools-support-eudr-2026-07-13_en)
