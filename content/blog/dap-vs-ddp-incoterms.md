A buyer in Europe asks for a quote "DDP". Your agent says DAP is safer. Both terms look almost the same on paper, and the difference can decide who gets a customs bill at the border.

DAP and DDP share nearly everything. They split on one point: who clears the goods into the importing country. This is the checklist we would run before you agree either term for a shipment into the EU. Seven checks, in the order they bite.

**At a glance:**

- Both terms put **delivery, transport and risk up to the named destination on the seller**. In both, the **buyer unloads**.
- The key difference is **import clearance**. Under **DAP the buyer** clears and pays. Under **DDP the seller** does.
- German customs calls DDP the **maximum obligation for the seller**.
- Some countries only let a **local importer** clear the goods. Then DDP does not work.
- The Incoterm also changes the **customs value** the importer declares.

## What DAP and DDP share

According to the [ICC Academy](https://academy.iccwbo.org/incoterms/article/incoterms-2020-dap-or-ddp/), the body behind the rules, both terms require the seller to deliver the goods at the named destination, ready for unloading, and to carry the risk of loss or damage until then. The seller arranges and pays the carriage and completes export formalities. The buyer takes delivery and unloads.

| | DAP | DDP |
|---|---|---|
| Carriage to destination | Seller | Seller |
| Export clearance | Seller | Seller |
| Risk passes | At the named destination | At the named destination |
| Unloading | Buyer | Buyer |
| Import clearance and duty | **Buyer** | **Seller** |

## 1. Start with the one difference: who clears import

Under DAP the buyer handles and pays the import clearance. Under DDP the seller delivers goods already cleared for import and pays the duties. German customs sums it up the same way: with DDP the seller clears the goods for export and import, pays the charges and completes the customs formalities. Everything else in this list follows from that one difference.

## 2. Check whether the seller can really import

DDP only works if the seller can act as importer in the destination country. The ICC warns that this may be difficult for a foreign seller, and that some customs authorities require the local buyer to clear the goods. In that case DAP is the right term.

For the EU, one number comes up first. A company outside the EU needs an EORI number if it lodges customs declarations itself, as we explain in [what an EORI number is](/blog/eori-number-checklist). If you export from India and agree DDP, you may be the one lodging them. Ask your customs agent in the destination country whether you can be the declarant, and on what conditions, before you quote.

## 3. Price the duty and taxes into a DDP offer

The ICC says a DDP buyer agrees to a higher price that covers the goods, packaging, transport from end to end, customs formalities and the tariffs, taxes and permits that customs requires. So a DDP quote has to include the duty. The duty depends on the product's code, so classify first. [How to find the right TARIC code](/blog/taric-code-checklist) shows how. German customs adds that the customs value is generally the basis for import VAT, so a wrong value moves two numbers at once. How import VAT is treated in your case is a question for your agent or tax adviser.

## 4. Understand who carries the risk during clearance

Under DAP, the ICC notes that the buyer bears the risk of loss or damage during import clearance (clause B3 a). If a buyer is not willing to take that risk, the ICC points to DDP instead. Under DDP the seller keeps the risk through clearance, but the buyer must help get any documents the import authorities ask for (clause B7).

## 5. Agree who unloads

In both terms unloading belongs to the buyer, unless the transport contract includes unloading and the seller pays for it. The buyer then carries only the risk during unloading. If you want the seller to unload, there is a separate rule, DPU. German customs says that under DPU the seller bears the cost and risk including unloading, while the buyer handles import and pays the import charges.

## 6. Check what it does to the customs value

The Incoterm is not only a commercial choice. German customs says that delivery terms are the starting point for deciding whether transport costs must be added to the price when the customs value is set. In general, transport and insurance costs are added up to the place where the goods enter the EU, if they are not already in the price, and costs incurred inside the EU can be deducted. With DAP, the buyer who files the import declaration needs the freight details from you. Send them with the invoice.

## 7. Write the term the way the ICC does

Name the rule, the exact place and the version: for example "DAP Hamburg, Incoterms 2020". The ICC still describes Incoterms 2020 as the latest edition. Use "DAP Hamburg" and the risk point is clear. Use "DAP Germany" and it is not.

## FAQ

**What is the difference between DAP and DDP?**
Under DAP the buyer clears the goods through import and pays the import charges. Under DDP the seller does both. In every other respect the two terms are alike.

Source: [ICC Academy: DAP and DDP explained](https://academy.iccwbo.org/incoterms/article/incoterms-2020-dap-or-ddp/)

**Who pays the import duty under DDP?**
The seller. German customs says DDP obliges the seller to clear the goods for export and import and to pay all charges for both.

Source: [Zoll: Lieferbedingungen (Incoterms 2020)](https://www.zoll.de/DE/Fachthemen/Zoelle/Zollwert/Methoden-der-Zollwertermittlung/Transaktionswert-fuer-die-eingefuehrte-Ware/Berichtigungen-Artikel-71-72-UZK/Befoerderungs-und-Versicherungskosten/Lieferbedingungen/lieferbedingung.html)

**Can a seller outside the EU use DDP for EU imports?**
It can be difficult. The ICC notes that some import customs authorities require the local buyer to clear the goods, and DAP then fits better. Ask your agent.

Source: [ICC Academy: DAP and DDP explained](https://academy.iccwbo.org/incoterms/article/incoterms-2020-dap-or-ddp/)

**Does the Incoterm affect the customs value?**
Yes. Delivery terms show whether transport costs must be added to the price. In general they are added up to the place of entry into the EU.

Source: [Zoll: Beförderungs- und Versicherungskosten](https://www.zoll.de/DE/Fachthemen/Zoelle/Zollwert/Methoden-der-Zollwertermittlung/Transaktionswert-fuer-die-eingefuehrte-Ware/Berichtigungen-Artikel-71-72-UZK/Befoerderungs-und-Versicherungskosten/befoerderungs-und-versicherungskosten_node.html)

**Which Incoterms edition is current?**
The ICC describes Incoterms 2020 as the latest edition. Name it in your contract.

Source: [ICC Academy: DAP and DDP explained](https://academy.iccwbo.org/incoterms/article/incoterms-2020-dap-or-ddp/)
