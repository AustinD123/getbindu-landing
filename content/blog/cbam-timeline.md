CBAM has no single deadline. It has a run of them, and the money part has not started yet.

Today, 13 November 2026, CBAM is in its definitive regime, but nobody has bought a certificate. Sales open on 1 February 2027. The first declaration is due on 30 September 2027, and it covers the goods imported during 2026.

**At a glance:**

- **1 January 2026:** the definitive regime began, after a transitional phase from 2023 to 2025.
- **50 tonnes a year:** the single mass threshold. Importers above it need authorised CBAM declarant status.
- **1 February 2027:** certificate sales open.
- **30 September 2027:** first CBAM declaration and certificate surrender, for 2026 imports.
- **1 January 2028:** proposed start of the extension to downstream goods. It is not law yet.

## The CBAM timeline at a glance

| Date | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 October 2023 to 31 December 2025 | Transitional phase: reporting only, no payment |
| 20 October 2025 | [Regulation (EU) 2025/2083](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/2083/oj) enters into force (the simplification) |
| 1 January 2026 | Definitive regime applies |
| 31 March 2026 | Cut-off to apply for authorised declarant status and keep importing meanwhile |
| 5 October 2026 | Commission publishes the Q3 2026 certificate price: €82.32 |
| 1 January 2027 | The quarterly holding rule starts; prices become weekly |
| 4 January 2027 | Q4 2026 price due |
| 1 February 2027 | Certificate sales open on the common central platform |
| 31 March 2027 | First quarter-end check of the 50% holding rule |
| 30 September 2027 | First declaration and surrender, for 2026 |
| 31 October 2027 | Deadline to ask to repurchase surplus certificates |
| 1 November 2027 | Certificates bought for 2026 emissions are cancelled |
| 1 January 2028 | Proposed extension to downstream goods |

The dates come from [Regulation (EU) 2025/2083](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/2083/oj) and the Commission's [CBAM page](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism/cbam-definitive-regime_en).

## 2023 to 2025: reporting without a bill

From October 2023 importers filed quarterly reports on embedded emissions. They paid nothing. The [CBAM report guide](/blog/cbam-report) covers what that filing looked like.

## 2026: the definitive regime, with no certificates yet

Since 1 January 2026, an importer who brings in more than 50 tonnes of covered goods in a calendar year must be an authorised CBAM declarant. The 50 tonnes is cumulative net mass for the year. It replaced the old €150-per-shipment rule.

An importer who applied by 31 March 2026 could keep importing while the authority decided. Anyone who missed that cut-off and has no status cannot import above the threshold.

There is no duty to hold certificates during 2026. The cost still builds up. The price of a certificate for 2026 imports is a quarterly average of EU carbon allowance auction prices, published by the Commission:

| Quarter | Published | Price per certificate |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 2026 | 7 April 2026 | €75.36 |
| Q2 2026 | 6 July 2026 | €75.28 |
| Q3 2026 | 5 October 2026 | €82.32 |
| Q4 2026 | 4 January 2027 | not yet published |

Each quarterly price applies to the goods you imported in that quarter. One certificate covers one tonne of CO2 equivalent. See [CBAM certificates](/blog/cbam-certificates) for how the purchase works.

## 2027: the first year you pay

From 1 January 2027 the Commission publishes a weekly price, not a quarterly one. From the same day, you must hold certificates equal to at least 50% of the emissions embedded in your imports so far that year, checked at the end of each quarter. The first check is 31 March 2027.

Certificates go on sale on 1 February 2027. The 2026 bill itself falls due on 30 September 2027, when you submit the CBAM declaration for 2026 and surrender the certificates in the same step. If you do not surrender enough, a penalty applies.

Two dates follow. By 31 October 2027 you can ask to repurchase surplus certificates. On 1 November 2027 the Commission cancels any certificates bought for 2026 emissions that remain in accounts. No compensation is paid.

## 2028: the downstream extension is only a proposal

The Commission proposed on 17 December 2025 to extend CBAM to about 180 downstream steel and aluminium goods from 1 January 2028. It is not adopted.

The Council agreed its position on 12 June 2026. Parliament adopted its own on 15 September 2026. Press reports say the first trilogue on 20 October produced no deal, and that the next round is on 30 November. Parliament's list is longer than the Commission's, so the final scope may change. Budget on today's scope until a text is adopted.

## What to do now

1. **Confirm your status.** Check that you hold authorised declarant status if you import more than 50 tonnes.
2. **Collect emissions data per shipment.** The September 2027 declaration needs it for every 2026 import. [Default values](/blog/cbam-default-values) apply when a supplier cannot provide it.
3. **Plan the cash.** Work out your running exposure at the current price before sales open.
4. **Put 31 March 2027 in your calendar.** It is the first holding check.
5. **Watch the trilogue.** If you import steel or aluminium goods, 2028 may change your scope.

## FAQ

**What is the CBAM timeline?**
CBAM ran a transitional phase from 2023 to 2025. The definitive regime applies from 1 January 2026. Certificates go on sale on 1 February 2027, and the first declaration is due on 30 September 2027.

Source: [European Commission: CBAM definitive regime](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism/cbam-definitive-regime_en)

**When is the first CBAM declaration due?**
On 30 September 2027, for imports made in 2026. The declaration and the surrender of certificates fall on the same date.

Source: [EUR-Lex: Regulation (EU) 2025/2083](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/2083/oj)

**When do CBAM certificates go on sale?**
From 1 February 2027, on a common central platform. Each Member State sells to the authorised declarants established in it.

Source: [EUR-Lex: Regulation (EU) 2025/2083](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2025/2083/oj)

**Do I have to hold CBAM certificates in 2026?**
No. The holding rule applies from 1 January 2027. In 2026 you only build up the obligation, priced at the quarterly average for the quarter of import.

Source: [European Commission: price of CBAM certificates](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism/price-cbam-certificates_en)

**Will CBAM cover more products from 2028?**
That is the proposal, from 1 January 2028. Council and Parliament have agreed positions, and talks are still running. Nothing is adopted yet.

Source: [Council of the EU: CBAM](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism/)
